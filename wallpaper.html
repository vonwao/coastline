A live coast as your wallpaper

This is a live simulation of the sea: waves, light and weather worked out as you watch, so it never repeats. A free app can put it behind your desktop icons, like a window onto the coast.

Try it in a new tab

Following a real beach uses Open-Meteo's free forecast. Only the beach's coordinates are sent, and the scene shows that beach's real swell, wind, cloud and time of day, not its real shape.

2a. On a Mac: Plash (free)

  1. Install Plash from the Mac App Store.
  2. Open it. It lives in the menu bar, at the top right of the screen.
  3. Choose Add Website, paste your link, and save.

2b. On Windows: Lively Wallpaper (free)

  1. Install Lively Wallpaper (also in the Microsoft Store).
  2. Click + (Add wallpaper), paste your link into the web address box, and confirm.
  3. Choose it as your wallpaper.

Good to know

  • It needs recent graphics: a Mac with an Apple chip, or a PC with Intel Arc, AMD Radeon 780M or better, or any gaming graphics card. Older laptops may stutter.
  • Power: on a recent mini PC it adds about 12 watts, about as much as a light bulb. On a laptop, use the app's settings to pause the wallpaper on battery or while a full-screen app is open.
  • Sound normally stays off in a wallpaper. To listen, open the link in a browser and press S.
  • Keys, in a browser tab: C for calm mode, B to pick a beach, M for the menu.
